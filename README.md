# Pump.fun Replay Sprint — Paper Bot

A deterministic momentum-sniper bot for the make.fun Pump.fun Replay Sprint. It trades 10 SOL of paper money on a 24-hour replay of pump.fun bonding-curve activity: it buys each fresh coin once (0.15 SOL) when early trading has grown its real reserve enough, then exits the full position on a 1.8x take-profit, a 0.65x stop-loss, a trailing stop (sell once the price has peaked at least 1.10x the entry price and fallen to 0.85x of that peak), or after 1500 slots — freeing its 5 slots to turn over many new coins and clear the 20-distinct-mint guardrail. All price comparisons use bigint cross-multiplication, and the bot never reads the clock, the environment, or Math.random, so its two grading runs reproduce byte for byte.
